About Nexus

We believe the objects in your home should be as intentional as the life you build around them.

Our Mission

Designed for the Way You Live

Nexus was founded on a simple belief: everyday objects deserve exceptional design. We partner with independent makers and studios around the world to curate a collection of modern essentials that bring clarity, comfort, and beauty to your daily rituals.

From our flagship studio in Brooklyn to our network of craftspeople across the globe, every piece in our collection is chosen with intention — for its design, its durability, and its ability to make your space feel more like home.
